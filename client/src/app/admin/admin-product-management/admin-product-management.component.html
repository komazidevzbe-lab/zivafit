ZivaFit Admin

Product Management

Manage the ZivaFit catalogue structure from one place. Products, categories, and stock variants belong here as inner tabs.

Products

This tab will manage the actual products being sold, such as leggings, sports bras, tops, sets, shorts, and accessories.

  • Product name
  • Product description
  • Product price in South African Rand
  • Product category
  • Product images
  • New In, Best Seller, Featured, Active / Inactive status
  • Created date and updated date

Categories

This tab will manage the product groups shown in the store and navbar.

  • Category name
  • Category description
  • Category image
  • Show or hide in navbar
  • Display order
  • Active / inactive status

Stock / Variants

This tab will manage the different size and colour combinations for each product.

  • Product
  • Colour
  • Size
  • Stock quantity
  • SKU if needed
  • Low stock and out of stock status
  • Active / inactive variant status
